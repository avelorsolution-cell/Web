Quotations

Quotations are prepared based on the information provided during our initial discussion. Prices are valid for 14 days unless stated otherwise, and may be revised if the scope of the project changes after the quotation is issued.

Scope of Work

The scope, deliverables, timeline and revision terms for each project are confirmed in writing before work begins. Work outside the agreed scope may require a separate quotation.

Deposits and Payments

Projects typically require an upfront deposit before work begins, with the balance due on delivery or according to agreed milestones. Payment terms are confirmed for each project individually.

Client Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for providing accurate project information, timely feedback, and any content, images or brand materials needed to complete the work. Delays in providing these may affect the project timeline.

Revisions

Each project includes an agreed number of revision rounds, confirmed at the quotation stage. Additional revisions beyond this may be quoted separately.

Timelines

Estimated timelines are provided at the start of each project. Timelines depend on the complexity of the work and on receiving client feedback and materials promptly.

Delays

Delays caused by late feedback, missing materials, or changes in scope may extend the agreed timeline. We will communicate any impact on delivery as early as possible.

Ownership of final deliverables transfers to the client upon full payment, unless otherwise agreed. AVELOR retains the right to display completed work in its portfolio unless the client requests otherwise.

Third-Party Costs

Costs such as domain registration, hosting, stock imagery, fonts or third-party licenses are not included in our fees unless stated in the quotation, and are billed separately or paid directly by the client.

Cancellation

If a project is cancelled after work has begun, the client is responsible for payment for work completed up to that point. Deposits already paid are non-refundable once work has started.

Maintenance

AVELOR is not a subscription-based company. Website maintenance is offered as one-time support or through a custom ongoing agreement confirmed separately with the client, and is never automatically included or billed without agreement.

Support After Delivery

Technical support and future improvements are available after delivery when required, either as a one-time paid engagement or under a separately agreed support arrangement.
